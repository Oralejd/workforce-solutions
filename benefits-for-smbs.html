Big benefits for SMBs using QuickBooks Workforce

QuickBooks Workforce connects you with best-in-class providers for retirement, health, HR, and workers' comp — all accessible directly through your QuickBooks account.

401(k) retirement plans
Powered by Human Interest

Offer employees a competitive retirement savings plan without the administrative burden. Human Interest integrates directly with QuickBooks Workforce for seamless contributions and recordkeeping.

Health benefits

Offer medical, dental, and vision coverage to attract and retain top talent. Plans are available for businesses of all sizes and integrate directly with your QuickBooks Workforce setup.

HR services

Access expert HR guidance, a customizable employee handbook builder, labor law compliance tools, and live HR advisor support — all included with Workforce Elite.

Workers' comp insurance
Powered by NEXT Insurance

Get affordable, flexible workers' compensation coverage that pays premiums based on actual payroll — no large upfront deposits or year-end audits. Syncs automatically with QuickBooks Workforce.

→ Explore workers' comp
All benefits are available directly through QuickBooks Workforce. You don't need separate accounts or platforms to offer these benefits. Everything is accessible from your QuickBooks Payroll dashboard. Visit quickbooks.intuit.com/payroll to see which benefits are included in your plan tier.
