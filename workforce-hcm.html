✦ What's New · May 2026

Meet the new HCM experience
in QuickBooks Workforce

Intuit launched QuickBooks Workforce on May 13, 2026 — bringing a full human capital management experience directly into Workforce Premium and Elite. Payroll, hiring, onboarding, performance, and benefits all in one place inside QuickBooks.

Small businesses typically rely on 7 to 25 separate tools to manage their workforce — creating fragmented data, manual data entry, and systems that don't talk to each other. QuickBooks Workforce consolidates all of this into a single hub connected directly to your books.
