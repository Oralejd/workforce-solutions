Scaling beyond QuickBooks Online

Intuit Enterprise Suite

For businesses that have outgrown QuickBooks Online but aren't ready for a traditional ERP. Intuit Enterprise Suite adds multi-entity consolidation, dimensional reporting, and integrated payroll & HR — built on the same foundation as QuickBooks, without a six-month implementation.

💡
Why businesses graduate to Intuit Enterprise Suite
🏢
Managing more than one entity

Subsidiaries, franchises, acquisitions, or related businesses that today require separate QuickBooks logins and manual report-stitching in Excel to see the full picture.

📊
Outgrowing spreadsheet-based reporting

When closing the books takes days instead of hours, and every board meeting means rebuilding reports by hand, it's a sign standard QBO reporting has hit its ceiling.

🔗
Intercompany transactions eating up staff time

Manually recording matching entries across entities is slow and error-prone. IES automates eliminations at the transaction level as they happen.

👥
Payroll, HR, and financials living in different systems

As teams grow, disconnected payroll/HR tools create duplicate data entry. IES brings QuickBooks Workforce Elite's full HCM suite into the same platform as the books.

🆕
What's included — Summer 2026 release
🧮
Automated multi-entity closeNew

Intercompany eliminations are generated and tracked automatically at the transaction level, and journal entries can be bulk-imported from spreadsheets — cutting close time from days to hours.

🗂️
Multi-level entity hierarchyNew

Customizable entity structures reflect how the business actually operates — not just its legal structure — with consolidated statements that roll up correctly either way.

📈
Dimensions & peer benchmarkingNew

Track financial activity across up to 20 custom dimensions — regions, departments, projects, product lines — well beyond classic classes and locations, plus real-time peer benchmarking.

👔
Integrated HCM (QuickBooks Workforce Elite)New

Hiring, onboarding, e-signatures, background checks (Checkr), employee documents, time off, performance management, and offboarding now run in the same environment as the financials.

🏗️
Construction EditionBeta

Intuit's first industry-specific IES vertical: cost groups for labor, materials, subcontractors, equipment, and overhead; project phases and budgets; estimating; AIA-style invoicing; e-signature proposals; and negative change orders. Also available as a paid add-on for QBO Advanced.

💬
Intuit Intelligence chatNew

Ask plain-language questions about the numbers — no report-building required — and get answers pulled directly from the business's live financial data.

🤖
AI-powered automation

Chart-of-accounts standardization, variance detection, and intercompany categorization run in the background so finance teams direct the work instead of doing it manually.

💱
Multi-currencyBeta

Exchange rates live in one permissioned rate table, applied automatically with full change logging and drill-down to the transaction level in any currency. Available now for U.S.-dollar functional currency organizations, with international currencies arriving this fall.

🔁
Cross-company bill payBeta

Record a bill payment in one entity using a bank account from another entity — intercompany accounting is generated and handled automatically behind the scenes.

Is Intuit Enterprise Suite the right fit?
Good fit for businesses that:
  • Manage multiple entities, locations, or subsidiaries
  • Are project-based — construction, specialty trades, professional services
  • Have outgrown spreadsheet-based consolidation and reporting
  • Want payroll, HR, and financials on one platform
Not yet the right fit if:
  • Inventory is core to the business — no serial/lot tracking, barcode workflows, or assembly builds as of 2026
  • The business needs global, heavy-manufacturing, or deep-customization ERP capability
  • Annual revenue is well under the ~$5M range QBO Advanced already serves well
Pricing is a custom quote based on user count, entity count, and integrations — Intuit does not publish flat pricing. A QSP Partner can help estimate cost and assess fit for a specific business.
