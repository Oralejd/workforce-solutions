Workers' compensation
Workers' comp is required in most states — and QuickBooks Workforce integrates with NEXT Insurance to offer a Pay As You Go option. Premiums are calculated automatically each payroll run based on actual wages, eliminating the year-end lump sum and simplifying your annual audit. The $5/mo administration fee applies to Workforce Payroll subscribers; the fee is waived for Workforce Premium and Workforce Elite. Note: integrated Pay As You Go is not available in OH, ND, WA, or WY — employers in those states must purchase coverage directly from the state fund.
